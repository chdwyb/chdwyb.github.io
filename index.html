Research & biography

Computer vision &
image restoration.

My research interests include computer vision, general image restoration, remote sensing image processing, unpaired learning, and multi-modal learning.

I am currently an Associate Professor in the School of Information Engineering, Chang'an University, where I conducted my postdoctoral research supervised by Prof. Shutao Li (李树涛), and received my Ph.D. degree supervised by Prof. Tao Gao (高涛). Before that, I worked as a visiting scholar at the School of Computing, Australian National University, where I conducted research with Senior Lecturer Jing Zhang (张静).
